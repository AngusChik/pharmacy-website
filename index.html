How It Works

Our Refill Process

We've made refilling your prescriptions as simple and stress-free as possible. Here's what to expect.

  • Submit Your Request

    Fill out our online refill form with your prescription details. You can also call us or visit in person.

  • Pharmacist Review

    Our pharmacist reviews your request, checks for interactions, and verifies refill eligibility with your prescriber if needed.

  • Preparation

    Your medication is carefully counted, labelled, and packaged. Every prescription is double-checked for accuracy.

  • Ready for Pickup

    We'll notify you when your prescription is ready. Come in at your convenience and our team will have it waiting.

Submit a Refill Request

What You'll Need

  • Your prescription number (found on your medication label)
  • Full name as it appears on the prescription
  • Date of birth for verification
  • Contact phone number
  • Name of the medication and dosage

Refill Turnaround

  • Standard refills: ready within 24 hours
  • Urgent requests: same-day when submitted before noon
  • New prescriptions may require additional processing time
  • We'll contact you if there are any delays

Good to Know

  • Refills can be requested online, by phone, or in person
  • We accept all major insurance plans
  • Ask our pharmacist about medication synchronization
  • Automatic refill reminders are available upon request
